Real light bending, not a video loop

Most black hole wallpapers are videos. This one is computed. The app traces a ray of light for every pixel through the spacetime of a spinning (Kerr) black hole, step by step. That ray tracing produces the shadow, the thin photon ring, the far side of the disk bent up over the top, and stars smeared into arcs near the Einstein ring.

The disk starts at the innermost stable orbit and turns at Kepler speed. The side moving toward you is brighter and bluer, from Doppler beaming and gravitational redshift.

Computed
Light paths, shadow, photon ring, the bent far side of the disk, Doppler brightening, lensed stars and objects.
Artistic
The colors. A real disk would glow white to orange. The palettes are a choice, and you can change them.

Seven presets, then make your own

Pick one per display or one for all. Duplicate any preset and change the hole, disk, colors, nebula, stars and edges.

Add things. The hole bends them.

Put a galaxy right behind the hole and it becomes a closed Einstein ring. Slightly off, it splits into two arcs. A planet on an orbit dips behind the disk and comes back bent. Comet tails point away from the hole.

Motion follows physics

  • →Bodies run on Kepler orbits. Fast visitors swing by or plunge in.
  • →Tides stretch them near the hole. Their light shifts red or blue.
  • →Moons stay locked to their planet. Asteroids tumble. Meteors slow down.
  • →Ships accelerate and bank. A second hole has real mass.
  • →A Time-lapse slider speeds it all up.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Free and open source under the MIT license.

Will it drain my battery?

It pauses in Low Power Mode and when windows cover the desktop. With only the edges moving it needs less than a tenth of the GPU time. On battery you can pick a lighter quality level. Numbers are in the README.

Which Macs does it run on?

macOS 15 or later.

Why does macOS warn me on first launch?

The app is not notarized by Apple. Since macOS 15, right-click and Open no longer helps. Click Open Anyway in System Settings > Privacy & Security once, or remove the quarantine flag with xattr. Homebrew quarantines its download too.

Is the physics real?

The light bending is: the app integrates light rays through Kerr spacetime. The colors are artistic. The README lists exactly what is real and what is approximated.

Can I use my own 3D models?

Yes: OBJ, USDZ, USD, PLY, STL, glTF and GLB.

Which languages does the app speak?

English and German. It follows the system language, or you pick one on the General page.

Does the app go online?

Only for updates. It asks api.github.com at most once a day and loads updates from GitHub's download servers. You can switch the check off on the General page. No analytics.

Does this website track me?

No. No cookies, no analytics, no requests to other servers.
